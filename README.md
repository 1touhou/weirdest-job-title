Entry for the [Weirdest Job Title: Blind Judge Showdown](https://make.fun/c/u-rusdyxbt-weirdest-job-title-blind-judge-showdown-552d7922) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun) — a blind judge compares job titles pairwise and picks the weirder, funnier one.

## The title

> **Lead Penguin Counter, Sahara Research Station**

45 characters. A real-sounding job with one hilariously specific twist: a penguin counter posted in the Sahara, where there are no penguins to count.

## How it works

`main.ts` is a deterministic Node 22 stdlib program — no randomness, no network, no dependencies. It writes exactly that one title, and nothing else, to `/output/entry.txt`.
